JPG → PDF

Scanned bills, ID photos or screenshots: DropMorph turns pictures into PDFs, and can put many pictures into one PDF with the Merge preset.

How to

JPG to PDF in three steps

1

Hold Ctrl and drag

Select your JPG file (or many), hold Ctrl (Option on macOS) and start dragging.

2

Drop on PDF

A donut of formats opens at your cursor. Drop the file on PDF.

3

Done

The new file is saved right next to the original. Prefer menus? Right-click the file → DropMorph.

FAQ

JPG to PDF: questions

How do I put several photos into one PDF?

Select them all, drag them with Ctrl held and drop them on Merge PDF.

Is there a file limit?

No limits. Everything runs on your PC.

Are my files uploaded?

No. DropMorph converts everything on your own computer and works offline.

Is DropMorph free?

Yes. Free and open source, with no sign-up, watermark or limits.
