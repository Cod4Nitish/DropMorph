How to

MP4 to MP3 in three steps

1

Hold Ctrl and drag

Select your MP4 file (or many), hold Ctrl (Option on macOS) and start dragging.

2

Drop on MP3

A donut of formats opens at your cursor. Drop the file on MP3.

3

Done

The new file is saved right next to the original. Prefer menus? Right-click the file → DropMorph.

FAQ

MP4 to MP3: questions

Why does it ask to download FFmpeg?

Video and audio use the FFmpeg engine. DropMorph downloads it once, from its official source, the first time you convert a video.

Can I convert many videos at once?

Yes. Select them all and drop them on MP3.

Are my files uploaded?

No. DropMorph converts everything on your own computer and works offline.

Is DropMorph free?

Yes. Free and open source, with no sign-up, watermark or limits.
