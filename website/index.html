How it works

Convert files in three steps

No window to open and no menu to dig through.

1

Hold a key and drag

Hold Ctrl (Option on macOS) and start dragging one file or a whole selection, from Explorer, the desktop or any folder.

2

Drop on a format

A donut opens at your cursor with only the formats that fit. Drop on PDF, JPG, MP3 or any other, or on a preset like Compress PDF.

3

Done, next to the original

A progress ring shows the work. The result is saved beside the original, and the original is never changed.

Command line & watch folders

Prefer typing? Or no typing at all?

The dropmorph command uses the same engines and presets as the app. Point a watch folder at your Downloads or a scanner's output and every new file is converted on its own.

Command line reference →

# one file
> dropmorph convert photo.heic --to jpg

# a whole folder, smaller files
> dropmorph convert *.png --to webp --quality 80 -o converted

# scans folder → PDFs, sub folders too
> dropmorph convert D:\Scans --recursive --preset to-pdf

# convert everything that lands in a folder
> dropmorph watch D:\Inbox --to pdf --move-originals

FAQ

Questions & answers

Is DropMorph really free?

Yes. DropMorph is free and open source under the MIT license. There's no account, no watermark, no trial and no limit on files.

Does it work offline? Are my files uploaded?

Everything is converted on your own computer and nothing is uploaded. DropMorph only goes online to check GitHub for a newer version and to download an optional engine you choose.

Which file formats does DropMorph support?

254 formats in 18 groups: images, PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, e-books, video, audio, subtitles, archives, CAD, fonts, data files, 3D models and map data. See the full list on the Formats page.

Do I need Microsoft Office or LibreOffice?

No. DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, PDF and most other formats work out of the box. Only older and rare formats such as DOC, PPT and Pages use the optional LibreOffice engine.

What are the optional engines?

Free programs DropMorph downloads only when you need them: FFmpeg for video and audio, LibreOffice for older office files, Pandoc for markup, Ghostscript for PostScript and calibre for rare e-books. Each is pinned to an official download and checked before use.

Does installing need administrator rights?

The Windows Setup asks for administrator permission once, and the portable ZIP runs without installing. On macOS and Linux DropMorph installs into your own user folder, without an administrator password.

Can I convert many files at once?

Yes. Select as many files as you like and drop them together; they convert in parallel. Watch folders convert every file that lands in them.

Can I convert from the Windows 11 right-click menu?

Yes. Setup can add DropMorph to the right-click menu, including the top of the compact Windows 11 menu.

Can it convert from the command line or automatically?

Yes. The dropmorph command converts files and folders, and dropmorph watch converts everything that lands in a folder. See the Command line page.

How do I convert a file, for example HEIC to JPG?

Hold Ctrl, drag the HEIC photo and drop it on JPG in the donut. The JPG is saved next to the original.

Why use DropMorph instead of an online converter?

Online converters upload your files to someone else's server, show ads and limit file sizes. DropMorph is faster, private, has no limits and works without internet.

Which operating systems are supported?

Windows 10 (1809 or newer) and Windows 11; macOS 14 Sonoma or newer, on Apple silicon (M1–M4) and Intel Macs; and Linux (Ubuntu 22.04 or newer and similar), on x64 and ARM64.

Can I use it on my phone?

DropMorph is a desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux; it doesn't run on Android or iPhone. You can open this site on your phone and share the download link to your computer.
